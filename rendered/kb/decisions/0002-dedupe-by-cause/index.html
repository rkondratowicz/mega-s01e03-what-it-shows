0002 · Match report causes, not subject lines

owner Rafal Kondratowicz with OpenCode proposed reviewed 2026-11-11 · 0 days ago

Date: 11 November 2026. Scope: this export review; desk adoption pending.

Context

Decision 0001 accepted a fast subject-only rule in 2024. It is preserved unchanged. The rendered examples now show that subject equality is neither necessary nor sufficient for sharing a cause. The #4471 review reconciles the merged reports against the tenant directory and classification code.

Proposed replacement

  1. Deduplicate export rows by report ID, not wording. Retain every distinct reporter record.
  2. Read the report, look up its tenant, and establish the channel and deployed form version.
  3. Use the actual tenant locale’s day/month order. Do not substitute browser locale or intake tag.
  4. Merge only when the evidence supports the same underlying cause as the destination ticket. For this export, sameCause must return true. A different cause needs its own ticket.
  5. Unknown tenant or unexplained symptoms: keep separate or unplaced and request the missing evidence. A matching subject cannot fill that gap.
  6. Count and link reports per cause. Recompute from the current data; check receipt and merge times separately. Recheck old known pages against the rendered views before relying on them.

Consequences

Triage costs more reading, but engineering no longer loses a smaller problem inside a large known ticket. Keep the two engineering issues linked from the review. Validate fixes separately; a parser change does not demonstrate that a booking-v3 hint is correct. Keep uncertainty visible instead of turning ambiguous bodies into an unsupported third cause.
